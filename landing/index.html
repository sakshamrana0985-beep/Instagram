The problem

400reels

That's roughly what a year of tapping the bookmark icon leaves you with: a folder of identical-looking thumbnails, and no memory of which one had the thing you actually needed.

The information was never lost — it was just locked inside video. You can't search it, skim it, or find it again without rewatching forty-five seconds you've already watched once.

The save felt productive. It produced nothing.

How it works

Three steps, no new app

Everything happens where you already forward things: Telegram. No account to create, nothing to install.

01

Forward the link

Instagram reel, YouTube video — paste or forward it to the bot. You'll get a "Saved ✓" back in under a second, no spinner.

02

Get a structured summary

The video is read, classified, and distilled — numbers, names, and steps kept exact, attributed to the original creator and post date.

03

Search it back up, months later

/search that thing about saving tax — finds it even if you can't remember the exact words, the account, or when you saved it.
